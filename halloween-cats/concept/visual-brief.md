## Shared design rules (apply to whichever concept is picked)

Product idea: fun, whimsical Halloween home decor. A chunky cat interacting with a Halloween item. Generic cats only, no licensed or trademarked characters.

Look and style
- Cute chunky designer-vinyl-toy or sticker look. Very simple, smooth, rounded forms.
- Solid flat color zones with crisp boundaries between neighboring colors. No gradients, no painterly shading, no texture, no fur detail, no glossy highlights, so the colors can be done by filament swaps or painting after printing.
- No dark outline strokes around shapes. Zones are separated only by the edge where two colors meet.
- Facial features (eyes, nose, mouth, carved pumpkin face) are bold simple shapes, never thin lines.
- The three concepts share one level of simplicity and chunkiness so they read as a product family, but each has a different cat pose, a different Halloween item, and a clearly different palette.

Printability
- Chunky, rounded, thick forms only. No whiskers, spindly legs, long or wispy tails, dangling bits, floating pieces, or tiny fragile details. Tails are short, thick, or tucked.
- Compact, self-supporting silhouette with a flat bottom that sits on a shelf on its own. A simple thick base is fine but not required. Avoid big overhangs.
- Every feature should still read as clearly thick at small display size.

Note on how the images render (applies to all three): the generator added soft, even "matte clay render" lighting, so you will see faint form shading on curved volumes (for example ribbing on the pumpkin, the inside of ears, the underside of hat brims). The COLOR ZONES themselves are flat. Treat the images as flat-color zone references and ignore the soft lighting when planning color regions. Concept B is rendered a touch flatter and more illustration-like than A and C, which have a slightly more 3D clay feel; they still read as one family.

## Concept A - "Pumpkin Hug"

- File: `H:\ClaudeStuff\3d-print-team\halloween-cats\concept\concept-a.png`
- Cat pose: chunky black cat SITTING upright, facing forward, with both front paws wrapped around the sides of the pumpkin. Hind feet are round mitten shapes at the bottom on either side of the pumpkin. Short thick tail curled around the right side.
- Halloween item: a big round jack-o'-lantern, nearly as wide as the cat's body, held against the cat's belly.

  - Pink: small nose
  - Orange: pumpkin body

- Description: a plump black cat with a big round head and two upright ears, hugging an oversized glowing-face pumpkin. The pumpkin face is the focal point and the yellow carving pops against the orange. The whole figure sits on a thick low round disc. This is the most "hug" and most symmetrical of the three, with a very stable, low center of gravity. Palette is dominated by black and orange, the most classic Halloween read.
- Printability concerns noticed in the image:
  - The model added a round base disc on its own. It is a good stability feature, but it was not requested, so the planner should decide whether to keep it.

  - The pumpkin stem overlaps the cat's chin/mouth area. A short thick stem is fine, but check that it stays clear of the chin.
  - The pumpkin has shallow vertical ribs. They are shading-level detail and could be simplified to a smooth surface or kept as very shallow grooves.
  - The pumpkin face is a raised or inset yellow color region, and its narrow teeth are the finest color detail in the design. The teeth may need to be kept chunky.
  - Large black mass means it will be a mostly one-color print with small color regions; dark filament hides fine surface detail.

- Printability concerns noticed in the image:

Caveats and inconsistencies noticed (for the Planner and modeler):

1. Pumpkin depth is not consistent between the two side views. In the left profile the pumpkin sticks out about 270 px in front of the belly; in the right profile about 370 px (roughly 8 percent of the frame width apart). The front view does not define this. Suggested reading: the pumpkin is a fat round ball, roughly as deep as it is wide, sitting on the base disc between the hind feet, with a good part of it in front of the belly and partly tucked behind the arms. When in doubt, follow the right-side and three-quarter views, which show the fuller ball.
2. The tail is the least consistent feature. Front: a rounded blob beside the hind foot. Back: a chunky crescent curl at the lower left. Left side: a thinner curled hook low at the rear (noticeably slimmer than the back-view tail). Top: a rounded knob at the disc edge on the right. What agrees across all views: exactly one tail, on the cat's left, low, resting near the base disc, short. The exact curl is the modeler's call; build it thick (the left-side view's slimmer version should NOT be followed, per the "no thin tails" rule).
3. The pumpkin is almost entirely hidden from behind. The cat's body covers it, and only tiny orange slivers show at the sides in the back view. So the "ribbed back of the pumpkin" is never really seen. Keep the pumpkin's rear plain orange and let it merge into the cat's belly and arms.
4. In the side views the chartreuse eye (and the pink nose) wrap around onto the side of the face and the whole eye is visible. In a true profile they would be nearly edge-on. Keep the eyes, nose and mouth on the front face only, as in the front view. The side views have no carved yellow on the pumpkin, as required.
5. Three-quarter view: "front-right" was read as the cat's front-right, matching the left/right naming above, so the camera sees the cat's right side and the tail is mostly hidden. A small rounded bump at the left edge behind the cat's right arm most likely is the rump or haunch (it could be read as a tail peeking out); there is only one tail, on the cat's left. If a view from the tail side is wanted, use `concept-a-side-left.png` or ask for a front-left three-quarter.
6. Top view: a tiny yellow sliver shows at the very front edge of the pumpkin (the carved grin peeking past the rim). From straight above, no yellow should be visible; ignore the sliver. The figure also sits closer to the front edge of the base disc than the front view suggests (the pumpkin nearly touches the disc edge), so position the figure on the disc from the front and side views, not the top view. The ears look splayed and wing-like from above; take their shape from the front and side views.
7. The earlier Concept A printability notes still apply to all views: tall ear tips are fragile, the thin smile line should be dropped or made bold, the stem sits close to the chin, and the base disc was an unrequested addition (all views show it, at the same thickness and diameter relative to the figure).
