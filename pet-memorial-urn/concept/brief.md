# Pet Memorial Urn — Concept Brief

**Approved final image:** `concept-v2.png`
**Supporting reference graphic:** `concept-v2-dimensions.png` (dimensions callout, styled after original product's own marketing graphic — see caveat below)

## Design description

A rounded, vase-like pet memorial urn in a matte tan/beige stoneware finish. The body has
a gently curved silhouette — wider through the belly, tapering inward to a narrower neck
at the top — echoing classic ceramic urn proportions. The front and sides of the body are
completely plain and smooth: no engraved plaque, no recessed panel, no name, no dates, no
paw-print icon, no text or graphics of any kind. This is a deliberate, permanent design
choice (personalization was explicitly removed from the concept — see iteration history).

The lid is domed, sized to seat on top of the neck opening, and finished in the same matte
stoneware look as the body. Centered on top of the lid is the key differentiating feature:
a thick, rounded, open-loop bail-style handle — substantial enough to hook a fingertip
under and lift, and open/through-hole in design (not an enclosed cavity), so it won't trap
unremovable 3D-print support material. It reads as an intentional, load-bearing part of
the lid, not a decorative nub.

## Iteration history / notes for the Planner

- **v1** established the core differentiator: took the reference commercial urn's shape
  and added the loop-handle lid, while keeping the reference's engraved "Milo" plaque for
  visual comparison purposes only.
- **v2 (approved)** made two changes, both now locked into the design:
  1. Removed the engraving/plaque entirely — the body is plain on all sides. Do not
     reintroduce a plaque, nameplate, or engraving area unless specifically requested again.
  2. The lid is shown separated from the body (resting beside it) purely to make both the
     open top of the urn and the handle on the lid independently visible for review. This
     is a presentation/staging choice for the concept image, not a design requirement —
     the actual product is a lid that seats on top of the body as normal (see the
     dimensions graphic, which shows the lid on, for the "assembled" view).
- The open-through-hole handle geometry (vs. an enclosed/blind loop) was specifically
  called out for print manufacturability (support-material removal) and should be
  preserved as a hard constraint when the Planner moves to CAD.

## Dimensions caveat (important)

The dimensions graphic (`concept-v2-dimensions.png`) displays Height 190 mm / 7.5 in,
Max Width 160 mm / 6.3 in, and Base Diameter 130 mm / 5.1 in, plus a note that handle
height adds to the 190 mm body figure. **These numbers are placeholder values copied
directly from the original commercial reference product**, used only because this
design's body proportions are intended to closely track that reference. They are not
independently measured or verified for the new design, and the handle's actual added
height has not been quantified at all. Establishing real, verified dimensions (including
wall thickness, lid fit tolerance, handle clearance, and total height with handle) is
`3d-planner`'s job, not something to treat as spec from this brief.
